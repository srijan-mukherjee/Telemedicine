# General Symptoms: Headache, Body Ache, Fatigue, Indigestion, Stomach Pain, Dizziness

**Specialty: General Medicine**

Most of us occasionally feel headaches, body aches, fatigue, indigestion, stomach pain, or dizziness. In healthy people these are usually short-lived and linked to stress, poor sleep, dehydration, skipped meals, or a passing viral illness. The value of this guide is knowing when such symptoms signal something more serious.

## Home care that helps

- **Headache:** Rest in a quiet, dark room, drink water, and keep a regular sleep schedule. Reduce caffeine and screen time if they seem to trigger pain.
- **Body ache and fatigue:** Gentle rest, hydration, and adequate sleep. If fatigue persists beyond normal illness recovery, look at sleep, stress, and diet.
- **Indigestion and stomach pain:** Eat smaller meals, avoid very spicy or greasy food, and sit upright after eating. Keep a note of foods that seem to trigger symptoms.
- **Dizziness:** Sit or lie down until it passes, avoid sudden standing, and drink fluids. If it keeps happening, mention it to your doctor.
- For pain or fever, use paracetamol only as per the doctor or the label.

## When to see a doctor

- Headaches that are frequent, unusual for you, or not relieved by rest.
- Ongoing fatigue, weight loss, or persistent stomach pain.
- Recurrent dizziness, especially with fainting or when it affects daily life.
- Symptoms that do not improve within a few days or keep returning.

## When to seek urgent care — RED FLAGS

**Get urgent help if a general symptom comes with any of these:**
- Sudden, severe "worst headache of your life" — seek emergency care.
- Headache with a stiff neck, high fever, confusion, weakness on one side, or difficulty speaking.
- Stomach pain that is severe, cannot be relieved, is getting worse, or comes with vomiting of blood or black, tarry stools.
- Dizziness with chest pain, severe shortness of breath, fainting, or a racing heartbeat.
- Sudden confusion, passing out, or loss of consciousness.

A one-off episode is usually harmless, but a sudden, severe headache, severe abdominal pain, or dizziness with chest symptoms deserves urgent evaluation rather than waiting at home.
